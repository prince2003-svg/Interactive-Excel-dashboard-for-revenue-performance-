Project Overview
This project processes and analyzes 2,339 global transaction records spanning international electronics sales across multiple regions, product lines, and sales representatives. The objective is to transition from raw transactional data to structured data hygiene, relational modeling, and dynamic executive reporting.
Architecture & Workbook Structure
The accompanying Excel workbook (Project_1_Global_Electronic_Sales.xlsx) is structured into a multi-layered analytical pipeline:
Raw_Data: The uncleaned extraction of global sales records, containing missing regional codes and formatting variations.
Sheet1 & Clean_Data: The staging and transformation layers where data anomalies are cleaned, columns are standardized, and derived dates/months are mapped.
Analysis: The aggregation layer utilizing Pivot Tables to slice performance metrics by Product Category, Country, and Month.
Dynamic_Dashboard: The presentation layer providing a command center view of global sales metrics.
Key Technical Skills Demonstrated
Data Cleaning & Hygiene: Standardizing multi-region transaction logs and addressing missing regional data fields.
Calculated Metrics: Formulating actual sales pricing, discount variances, quantity totals, and aggregate revenue.
Dimensional Aggregation: Grouping high-volume global transactions into actionable categories for international reporting.
Executive Dashboarding: Structuring visual layers for macro-level trend analysis across international markets.
Key Insights & Findings
Total Scale: The dataset accounts for $34,053,377 in total gross revenue across 116,499 units sold.
Top Performing Market: The USA stands as the leading geographic market, generating $4,514,744 in total revenue.
Top Product Category: Refrigerators represent the highest-grossing product line globally, driving $7,017,020 in revenue.
